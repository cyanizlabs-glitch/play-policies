Lumewalk™ Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026 · Publisher: Moe Will

Not Model Fest. Lumewalk is a local board for industry events, castings, and call sheets. It is not a fashion-week event brand, festival, or ticket marketplace.

Local / demo build

This Play closed-test build is designed to keep your board on the device you are using. Events, castings, call-board notes, and similar entries are saved in on-device storage. The first launch may include sample demo data. That seed data is not a live production calendar. Cyaniz Labs does not operate a Lumewalk cloud account in this version, and the app does not require an online login.

What we do not collect

Moe Will / Lumewalk does not run a backend in this build that receives your board records. We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs in this app.

What you enter

Notes you keep about walks, lights, books, or call times stay local unless you export them yourself. Clearing app data or uninstalling Lumewalk deletes the local store on that device.

Children

Lumewalk is intended for adult industry professionals. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and the in-app Privacy screen.
