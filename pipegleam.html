Pipegleam™ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs, LLC

Productivity — plumbing field documentation. Pipegleam™ helps crews capture job photos, checklists, signatures, and proof summaries on-device. It is not a medical app and not a substitute for licensed contractor judgment. Not legal advice.

Information we collect

Pipegleam™ may store job photos, checklist progress, signatures, and notes you enter. Typically this stays in on-device storage for this build. This privacy page itself does not collect app data.

How we use it

Data you enter is used only to operate Pipegleam™ for you (field documentation and optional exports you choose). We do not sell your personal information.

Third parties

We do not share your operational records with third parties except service providers strictly necessary to run the app (for example Google Play Billing for optional purchases), under their policies. This build does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children

Pipegleam™ is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
