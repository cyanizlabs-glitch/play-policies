Pipegleam™

Cyaniz Labs™ · Package com.pipegleam.app · Play category: Productivity

Productivity app for Plumbing field documentation. Pipegleam™ helps solo and small crews capture before/during/after job photos, run trade checklist packs, collect customer signatures on change-orders, and export a shareable proof summary — all stored on-device. It is not a medical app, not clinical software, and not a substitute for licensed contractor judgment or code authority. Not legal advice.

Store listing framing

  • Category: Productivity (business / field documentation tools)
  • Audience: licensed or apprenticing plumbing technicians and small crews
  • No health, therapy, or medical claims
  • Photos and signatures stay local unless the user exports/shares them

Privacy summary

See also in-app privacy.html. Job media and signed addenda are local-first. Optional Premium uses Google Play Billing. No ad SDKs in this build.
