Sponsor Proof Live Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs / Moe Will

Simulated demonstration data only. Sponsor Proof Live is a contract-to-proof demo for livestream sponsorship campaigns. This Play build uses simulated records. It is not a production measurement system and does not process live campaign data.

What this app stores

This demo is designed to keep sample records on the device you are using. Campaign names, evidence placeholders, and similar entries in this build are simulated demonstration data, not real advertiser, creator, or viewer information. Cyaniz Labs does not operate a cloud account for this demo, and the app does not require an online login.

What we do not collect

Cyaniz Labs does not run a backend in this demo that receives your records. We do not sell personal information. We do not include advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs in this app. This listing is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or partnered with any livestreaming platform.

Permissions

  • Internet — used by the Android WebView to load the bundled app. This demo does not send your records to a Sponsor Proof Live server.

Children

Sponsor Proof Live is intended for adult media, advertising, and operations professionals. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
