Custkeep™ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs, LLC

Not legal advice. Not a lawyer. Custkeep is a family-court evidence helper for timestamped records you keep yourself. It is not a lawyer, not a court filing system, and not a substitute for professional legal advice.

Information we collect

Photos, message exports, notes, and timestamps are saved in on-device storage. First launch may include sample demo data that is not a real case file.

How we use it

Data you enter is used only to operate Custkeep for you. We do not sell personal information. Keep your original photos, messages, and documents in your own files.

Third parties

Cyaniz Labs, LLC does not run a backend in this build that receives your evidence records. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children

Custkeep is intended for adults managing their own records. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
