Custkeep Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026 · Publisher: Moe Will

Not legal advice. Not a lawyer. Custkeep is a family-court evidence helper for timestamped records you keep yourself. It is not a lawyer, not a court filing system, and not a substitute for professional legal advice. You keep the originals.

Local / demo build

This Play closed-test build is designed to keep records on the device you are using. Photos, message exports, notes, and timestamps are saved in on-device storage. The first launch may include sample demo data. That seed data is not a real case file. Cyaniz Labs does not operate a Custkeep cloud account in this version, and the app does not require an online login.

What we do not collect

Moe Will / Custkeep does not run a backend in this build that receives your evidence records. We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs in this app. Do not treat this prototype’s storage as production-grade evidence archival.

Originals stay with you

Custkeep is a helper for organizing copies and timestamps. Keep your original photos, messages, and documents in your own files. If you export a pack, that file lives wherever you save or share it. Treat it as sensitive household and legal information. Clearing app data or uninstalling Custkeep deletes the local store on that device; it does not delete originals you kept elsewhere.

Children

Custkeep is intended for adults managing their own records. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and the in-app Privacy screen.
