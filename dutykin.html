Dutykin™ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs, LLC

Not a medical device. Dutykin is a family caregiver organizer. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor medical conditions, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice. In a true emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency number) immediately.

Information we collect

Dutykin is designed to keep care notes on the device you are using. Routine items, medications, vitals logs, appointments, family-circle messages, and similar entries are saved in on-device storage. This build does not require an online login.

How we use it

Data you enter is used only to operate Dutykin for you. We do not sell personal information.

Third parties

Cyaniz Labs, LLC does not run a backend in this build that receives your care records. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs. Phone links open the device dialer; optional location in help/SOS flows is not uploaded to a Dutykin server.

Children

Dutykin is intended for adult family caregivers. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
