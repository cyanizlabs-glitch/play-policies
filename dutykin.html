Dutykin™ Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026 · Publisher: Moe Will

Not a medical device. Dutykin is a family caregiver organizer. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor medical conditions, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice. In a true emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency number) immediately.

What Dutykin stores

Dutykin is designed to keep care notes on the device you are using. Routine items, medications, vitals logs, appointments, family-circle messages, and similar entries are saved in on-device storage (for example, the app’s local storage). We do not operate a Dutykin cloud account, and this app does not require you to create an online login.

What we do not collect

Moe Will / Dutykin does not run a backend that receives your care records. We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs in this app.

Permissions and optional device features

  • Phone links (for example, Call 911) open the device dialer. Dutykin does not place calls by itself.
  • If you use the help/SOS flow, the app may ask the device for a location so you can share it. Location is not uploaded to a Dutykin server.
  • Voice notes use on-device speech features when the platform provides them. If a microphone or speech API is unavailable, the app falls back to a local placeholder.

Backups you create

If you export a JSON backup or print a care summary, that file lives wherever you save or share it. Treat it as sensitive household information.

Children

Dutykin is intended for adult family caregivers. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and the in-app Privacy screen.
