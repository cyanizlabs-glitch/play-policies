Hood Reaver Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026 · Publisher: Moe Will

Game MVP (closed test). Hood Reaver is an early urban-fantasy stealth game prototype. Progress in this build is local or demo-only.

Local / demo build

This Play closed-test MVP is designed to keep play state on the device you are using. Settings, progress, and similar entries are saved in on-device storage. The first launch may include sample demo content. Cyaniz Labs does not operate a Hood Reaver cloud account in this version, and the app does not require an online login.

What we do not collect

Moe Will / Hood Reaver does not run a backend in this build that receives your play data. We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs in this app. There are no in-game purchases in this MVP that send payment data to Cyaniz Labs.

What stays on device

Gameplay records stay local unless you later opt into an online feature we disclose here first. Clearing app data or uninstalling Hood Reaver deletes the local store on that device.

Children

Hood Reaver is intended for adult players. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and the in-app Privacy screen.
