Aidgleam™ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs, LLC

Not a medical device. Not medical advice. Aidgleam™ is an informational caregiver benefits and resource navigator with optional checklists. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor health conditions. In an emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency number) immediately.

Information we collect

Quiz answers, checklist progress, and preferences stay on the device (local storage). Closed testing may use an on-device account stored only on this device.

How we use it

Data you enter is used only to operate Aidgleam™ for you. We do not sell personal information.

Third parties

Optional Premium is processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details; Cyaniz Labs, LLC receives purchase entitlement signals, not your full card numbers. We do not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children

Aidgleam™ is intended for adult caregivers and family members. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
