Aidgleam™ Privacy Policy

Effective September 10, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs / Moe Will

Not a medical device. Not medical advice. Aidgleam™ is an informational caregiver benefits and resource navigator with optional checklists. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor health conditions and is not a substitute for professional advice. In an emergency, call 911 (or your local emergency number) immediately.

Local-first

Quiz answers, checklist progress, and preferences stay on the device (local storage). Cyaniz Labs does not operate an Aidgleam™ cloud account in this version. Closed testing may use an on-device account (email/password stored only on this device); free access continues after local sign-in, and premium remains optional via Play Billing.

What we do not collect

We do not sell personal information. We do not include advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs in this app. We do not run a backend in this build that receives your checklist or quiz records.

Purchases

Optional Premium (subscription or unlock) is processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details under Google’s policies. Cyaniz Labs receives purchase entitlement signals through Play, not your full payment card numbers.

Permissions

  • Internet — used by the Android WebView to load the bundled Capacitor app and by Google Play Billing.
  • Billing — used only for optional Premium products.

Children

Aidgleam™ is intended for adult caregivers and family members. It is not directed at children under 13. Do not enter sensitive personal information about a minor into the app.
