Burewright™ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs, LLC

Not a licensed advisor. Not credit repair. Not a lender. Burewright is a personal and business credit-file craft tool. It does not provide credit counseling, credit-repair services, loan offers, or guaranteed score results.

Information we collect

Tradeline trackers, utilization notes, and similar entries are saved in on-device storage. First launch may include sample demo data that is not real credit-file information.

How we use it

Data you enter is used only to operate Burewright for you. We do not sell personal information.

Third parties

Cyaniz Labs, LLC does not run a backend in this build that receives your credit-file notes. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not pull official bureau reports through this prototype.

Children

Burewright is intended for adults managing their own personal or business credit files. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
