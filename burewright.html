Burewright™ Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026 · Publisher: Moe Will

Not a licensed advisor. Not credit repair. Not a lender. Burewright is a personal and business credit-file craft tool. It does not provide credit counseling, credit-repair services, loan offers, or guaranteed score results. You decide what to do with your own files.

Local / demo build

This Play closed-test build is designed to keep your notes on the device you are using. Tradeline trackers, utilization notes, and similar entries are saved in on-device storage. The first launch may include sample demo data so you can try the modules. That seed data is not real credit-file information. Cyaniz Labs does not operate a Burewright cloud account in this version, and the app does not require an online login.

What we do not collect

Moe Will / Burewright does not run a backend in this build that receives your credit-file notes. We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs in this app. We do not pull official bureau reports through this prototype.

What you enter

Anything you type about personal or business credit files stays local unless you export it yourself. Treat exported notes as sensitive financial information. Clearing app data or uninstalling Burewright deletes the local store on that device.

Children

Burewright is intended for adults managing their own personal or business credit files. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and the in-app Privacy screen.
