Spryhaul™ Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs / Moe Will

Not a medical device. Not HIPAA. Spryhaul is a business logistics app for sprinters, cargo vans, and box trucks. It does not diagnose, treat, handle specimens, or certify collectors. It is not a HIPAA-covered product and is not represented as HIPAA-certified.

Spryhaul ("we," "us," "our") provides freight matching and day-sheet tools for small carriers. Tagline: The mile that pays. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how to request deletion.

Closed-testing / demo build

The current Play closed-testing build runs with a bundled frontend and demo/seed data. No production backend URL is configured (server.url is empty). Records stay on the device (or in the bundled demo). Cyaniz Labs does not operate a live Spryhaul cloud account for you in this version.

Information we collect

  • Account data: company name, work email, and password (stored hashed) if you create an account on a later hosted backend.
  • Operational data you enter: vehicles, loads, dispatches, contracts, credentials, and notes you create to run your freight business.
  • Device data: standard technical data (device type, app version) for crash diagnostics. We do not collect precise background location; any location use is limited to data you actively enter (city/state) unless a future release adds device location and discloses it here first.

How we use it

Data you enter is used only to operate the app for you: matching vehicles to loads, keeping a day sheet, and generating operational records. We do not sell your data. We do not share operational data with third parties except service providers strictly necessary to run the app (e.g., hosting), under contractual confidentiality.

Permissions

  • Internet — used by the Android WebView to load the bundled Capacitor app (local origin). This closed-test build does not send records to a Spryhaul server.

Data retention & deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below. We will delete or anonymize your data within 30 days of a verified request, except where retention is required by law. Clearing app data or uninstalling Spryhaul deletes the local store on that device.

Security

When a backend is used, data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No system is 100% secure; we continuously review our controls.

Children

Spryhaul is a business tool intended for adult commercial carrier operators and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and the in-app Privacy screen.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: Cyaniz Labs / Moe Will. cyanizlabs@gmail.com
