Spryhaul Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026 · Publisher: Moe Will

Not a medical device. Not HIPAA-certified. Spryhaul is a freight-matching and dispatch tool for small vans, sprinters, and box trucks. It does not diagnose, treat, handle specimens, or provide medical advice. It is not a HIPAA-covered product and is not represented as HIPAA-certified.

Closed-testing / demo build

This Play closed-test build is a prototype. It may ship with demo or seed data and an empty or unset backend URL. In that mode, records stay on the device (or in the bundled demo) and Cyaniz Labs does not operate a live Spryhaul cloud account for you. If you later host a backend yourself and point the app at it, this policy still applies to data you choose to submit there — we do not host that backend unless you separately arrange it.

What Spryhaul stores

Spryhaul is designed to keep operational records on the device you are using unless you connect a backend you host. Vehicles, loads, day sheets, and similar entries are saved in on-device storage (for example, the app’s local storage). Demo data is not real customer information. We do not require you to create an online login for this prototype.

What we do not collect

Moe Will / Spryhaul does not run a production backend that receives your freight records in this build. We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs in this app.

How it is used

Data you enter is used only to operate the app for you: matching vehicles to loads, keeping a day sheet, and drafting dispatch notes. A copilot, if present, may draft text — it does not post without you. We do not sell your data.

Children

Spryhaul is intended for adult commercial operators. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and the in-app Privacy screen.
