BiteRover Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs / Moe Will

Food-cart app (Trusted Web Activity). BiteRover is a food-cart discovery and ordering app distributed on Google Play as an Android Trusted Web Activity. Package: com.biterover.app. This page is the public privacy policy for the Play listing.

About the web origin

The Android app is built as a Trusted Web Activity intended to load the BiteRover product from the domain biterover.com. That domain is not live yet. Until it is deployed, this GitHub Pages URL is the privacy policy Play Console should use. This page does not claim that biterover.com is currently serving the product, accepting orders, or collecting account data.

What BiteRover is designed to do

When the product web origin is deployed, BiteRover is meant to help people find nearby food carts, order ahead, and earn rewards. Payments, if enabled, would go through Stripe-hosted checkout so card numbers never touch Cyaniz Labs servers. Until the web origin is live, an installed TWA cannot load the product and does not operate a live marketplace from this policy page.

What we collect

Cyaniz Labs does not collect information through this privacy-policy page. We do not sell personal information. This Play listing’s TWA wrapper does not include advertising SDKs.

When the product service is later deployed, the live app may collect account contact details (such as email), order history, and optional location to show nearby carts — only as needed to run the service. Card data would be handled by Stripe, not stored by Cyaniz Labs. A separate data-deletion path will be published with the live product; until then, contact us at the email below to request deletion of any account data we hold.

Children

BiteRover is intended for adults ordering food. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. When biterover.com is live, we may also host a product privacy policy there and keep this page as a redirect or copy.

Contact

Questions: Cyaniz Labs / Moe Will, publisher of BiteRover. Contact: cyanizlabs@gmail.com.
