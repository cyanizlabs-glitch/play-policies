BiteRover™ Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs / Moe Will

Food-cart discovery app. BiteRover (package com.biterover.app) is a food-cart discovery and ordering organizer on Google Play. Closed-test builds from 1.0.1 ship as a bundled Capacitor app so the UI works without relying on a live biterover.com host. This page is the public privacy policy for the Play listing.

What BiteRover stores

The bundled closed-test build keeps demo and on-device UI state on your device. Cyaniz Labs does not collect information through this privacy-policy page. We do not sell personal information. The Play wrapper does not include advertising SDKs.

Purchases

If premium access or subscriptions are offered inside BiteRover, payment is processed by Google Play. Google handles payment details; Cyaniz Labs receives purchase status needed to unlock features, not your full card number. Stripe-hosted checkout may be used later for food orders so card numbers never touch Cyaniz Labs servers.

Optional future web origin

A production deploy may later use biterover.com. Until that domain serves a stable HTTPS product, this GitHub Pages URL remains the Play privacy policy. Contact us below to request deletion of any account data we hold.

Children

BiteRover is intended for adults ordering food. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. When biterover.com is live, we may also host a product privacy policy there and keep this page as a redirect or copy.

Contact

Questions: Cyaniz Labs / Moe Will, publisher of BiteRover™. Contact: cyanizlabs@gmail.com.
