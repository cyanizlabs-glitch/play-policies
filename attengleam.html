Attengleam™ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs, LLC

Not a medical device. Attengleam™ is a kid-safe focus-training practice app (breathing guides, streaks, mentor tokens, simple games). It is not an ADHD treatment app and does not diagnose, treat, or monitor health conditions.

Information we collect

Practice stats, streaks, tokens, and preferences stay on the device (local storage). This build does not require an online login.

How we use it

Data you enter is used only to operate Attengleam™ for you. We do not sell personal information.

Third parties

Optional Premium is processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details; Cyaniz Labs, LLC receives purchase entitlement signals, not your full card numbers. We do not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Children

Attengleam™ is designed for family use with a parent gate for the parent zone. Parents should supervise use. Do not enter sensitive personal information about a child into the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
