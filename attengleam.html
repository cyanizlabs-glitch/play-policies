Attengleam™ Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs / Moe Will

Not a medical device. Attengleam™ is a kid-safe focus-training practice app (breathing guides, streaks, mentor tokens, simple games). It is not an ADHD treatment app and does not diagnose, treat, or monitor health conditions. For family practice and encouragement only.

Local-first

Practice stats, streaks, tokens, and preferences stay on the device (local storage). Cyaniz Labs does not operate an Attengleam™ cloud account in this version, and the app does not require an online login.

What we do not collect

We do not sell personal information. We do not include advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs in this app. We do not run a backend in this build that receives your practice records.

Purchases

Optional Premium (subscription or unlock) is processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details under Google’s policies. Cyaniz Labs receives purchase entitlement signals through Play, not your full payment card numbers.

Permissions

  • Internet — used by the Android WebView to load the bundled Capacitor app and by Google Play Billing.
  • Billing — used only for optional Premium products.

Children

Attengleam™ is designed for family use with a parent gate for the parent zone. Parents should supervise use. Do not enter sensitive personal information about a child into the app.
