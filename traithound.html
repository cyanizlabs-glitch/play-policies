TraitHound™ Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs / Moe Will (TraitHound™)

Canine breeding / pedigree organizer. TraitHound (package com.manifestedgenius.k9codex) helps breeders and owners organize dog pedigree and breeding records on Google Play. It is not a veterinary diagnostic device and does not replace professional veterinary care.

What TraitHound stores

TraitHound is designed so breeding notes, pedigrees, and related records you enter stay in the app’s storage on your device and/or accounts you connect inside the product. Cyaniz Labs does not sell your personal information. This privacy page itself does not collect breeding data.

Purchases and subscriptions

If you buy premium access or subscriptions inside TraitHound, payment is processed by Google Play. Google handles payment details; Cyaniz Labs receives purchase status needed to unlock features, not your full card number.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information for advertising. We do not use this policy page to run ads. TraitHound is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and keep a public copy at this URL for the Play listing.
