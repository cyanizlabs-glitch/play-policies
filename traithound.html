TraitHound™ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs, LLC

Canine breeding / pedigree organizer. TraitHound (package com.manifestedgenius.k9codex — legacy package; publisher is Cyaniz Labs, LLC) helps breeders and owners organize dog pedigree and breeding records on Google Play. It is not a veterinary diagnostic device and does not replace professional veterinary care.

Information we collect

TraitHound is designed so breeding notes, pedigrees, and related records you enter stay in the app’s storage on your device and/or accounts you connect inside the product. This privacy page itself does not collect breeding data.

How we use it

Data you enter is used only to operate TraitHound for you. We do not sell your personal information.

Third parties

If you buy premium access or subscriptions, payment is processed by Google Play. Google handles payment details; Cyaniz Labs, LLC receives purchase status needed to unlock features, not your full card number. We do not use advertising SDKs on this privacy page.

Children

TraitHound is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
