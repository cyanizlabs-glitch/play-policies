Scenloom™ Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026 · Publisher: Moe Will

Not NVIDIA Omniverse. Scenloom is an AI studio for individual scenes. It is not NVIDIA Omniverse, not a universe platform, and not affiliated with NVIDIA.

Local / demo build

This Play closed-test build is designed to keep your scene notes and previews on the device you are using. Prompts, scene drafts, and similar entries are saved in on-device storage. The first launch may include sample demo data. Cyaniz Labs does not operate a Scenloom cloud account in this version, and the app does not require an online login.

What we do not collect

Moe Will / Scenloom does not run a backend in this build that receives your scene files. We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs in this app.

What you create

Scenes you weave stay local unless you export them yourself. If a later release calls an optional model API you choose to enable, we will update this page before that happens. This prototype does not claim to process your work on Cyaniz Labs servers.

Children

Scenloom is intended for adult creators. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and the in-app Privacy screen.
