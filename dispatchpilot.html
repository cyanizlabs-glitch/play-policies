DispatchPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs / Moe Will

Not a medical device. Not HIPAA-certified. DispatchPilot is a business logistics and dispatch operations tool for carriers and dispatchers. It does not diagnose, treat, interpret lab or drug-test results, certify collectors, or provide medical advice. It is not a HIPAA-covered product and is not represented as HIPAA-certified.

DispatchPilot ("we," "us," "our") provides freight-matching, fleet, and courier-workflow software for small carriers. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and how to request deletion.

Information we collect

  • Account data: company name, work email, and password (stored hashed) if you create an account on a hosted backend.
  • Operational data you enter: vehicles, loads, dispatches, contracts, credentials, and operational notes you create to run your business.
  • Courier / chain-of-custody workflow data: if you use stop or chain-of-custody features, the app records stop type, timestamps, and entries you or your driver enter. These are operational logistics records, not patient medical records.
  • Device data: standard technical data (device type, app version) for crash diagnostics. We do not collect precise background location; any location use is limited to data you actively enter (city/state) unless a future release explicitly adds device location and discloses it here first.

Closed-testing / demo build

The current Play closed-testing build may run with a bundled frontend and demo/seed data, with no production backend URL configured. In that mode, records stay on the device (or in the bundled demo) and Cyaniz Labs does not operate a live DispatchPilot cloud account for you. If you later point the app at a hosted backend, this policy still applies to data you submit there.

How we use it

Data you enter is used only to operate the app for you: matching loads, tracking dispatches, optimizing routes, generating rate confirmations and proposals, and maintaining your operational records. We do not sell your data. We do not share operational data with third parties except service providers strictly necessary to run the app (e.g., hosting), under contractual confidentiality.

HIPAA and compliance status

DispatchPilot is not HIPAA-certified, is not a medical device, and is not independently compliance-audited. Chain-of-custody and courier features are workflow controls for your logistics operations, not a substitute for your own regulatory, DOT, or privacy obligations. Organizations that handle protected health information must confirm their own compliance before using any software in a regulated workflow.

Data retention & deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below. We will delete or anonymize your data within 30 days of a verified request, except where retention is required by law.

Security

When a backend is used, data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). No system is 100% secure; we continuously review our controls.

Children's privacy

DispatchPilot is a business tool intended for adult commercial carrier operators and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and update the "Last updated" date above.
