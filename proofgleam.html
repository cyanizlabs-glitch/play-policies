Proofgleam™ Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs, LLC

Not a medical device. Proofgleam is a cleaning-operations organizer (schedules, checklists, proof-of-clean photos, quotes, and invoices). It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor health conditions, and it is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, or tax advice.

Information we collect

Jobs, customers, photos, signatures, and similar entries are saved in on-device storage in this build. First launch may include sample demo data that is not real customer information.

How we use it

Data you enter is used only to operate Proofgleam for you. We do not sell personal information.

Third parties

Cyaniz Labs, LLC does not run a backend in this build that receives your operational records. We do not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Camera/photos are used only when you attach proof photos.

Children

Proofgleam is intended for adult cleaning-business operators and crews. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
