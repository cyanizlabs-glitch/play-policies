Proofgleam Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026 · Publisher: Cyaniz Labs / Moe Will

Not a medical device. Proofgleam is a cleaning-operations organizer (schedules, checklists, proof-of-clean photos, quotes, and invoices). It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor health conditions, and it is not a substitute for professional medical, legal, or tax advice.

Local-first prototype

This build of Proofgleam is designed to keep your records on the device you are using. Jobs, customers, photos, signatures, and similar entries are saved in on-device storage (the app’s local storage). Cyaniz Labs does not operate a Proofgleam cloud account in this version, and the app does not require you to create an online login.

Seed / demo data

The first launch includes sample demo data (a fictional cleaning company, crews, customers, jobs, invoices, and related records) so you can try every module. That seed data is not real customer information. Anything you edit is stored locally. Clearing app data or uninstalling Proofgleam deletes the local store on that device.

What we do not collect

Cyaniz Labs does not run a backend in this build that receives your operational records. We do not sell personal information. We do not include advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs in this app.

Photos and signatures

Field proof photos are read on-device with the system file picker / camera (FileReader) and stored as local data URLs. Customer signatures stay on the device. Obtain client consent before photographing occupied spaces. Do not treat this prototype’s storage as production-grade evidence archival.

Permissions

  • Internet — used by the Android WebView to load the bundled app (Capacitor’s local HTTPS origin). This build does not send your records to a Proofgleam server.
  • Camera / photos — used only when you attach a before/after photo for a proof pack. Gallery access uses the system picker. Camera is optional; you can use a sample photo instead.

Children

Proofgleam is intended for adult cleaning-business operators and crews. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and the in-app Privacy screen.
